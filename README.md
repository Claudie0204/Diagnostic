# Factory Operations Diagnostic

I am developing a tool to help understand how a factory operates, identify what affects its performance, and determine where further investigation or improvement is needed.
This project draws on my experience in industrial operations and supply chain management. I am also learning data analysis to better understand how data, analytical methods, and software can support an operational diagnosis.

## Starting with the business problem
The starting point is understanding how work gets done: how teams set priorities, manage disruptions, make decisions, and coordinate across functions.
A factory may have documented processes and an ERP system, while daily operations also depend on spreadsheets, informal routines, and individual knowledge. Understanding the differences between these sources is part of the diagnosis.

## The approach
The tool is intended to support a structured assessment combining:
- Business objectives and operational performance.
- Reliable execution of everyday activities.
- Roles, responsibilities, and decision-making authority.
- Dependencies between teams and competing priorities.
- Data quality, system usage, and manual workarounds.
- Recurring problems, escalation practices, and how issues are resolved.

The approach starts with the basics: a reliable way of running daily operations makes deviations easier to identify. Examining those deviations helps reveal where teams need support, where decisions require escalation, and where the operating model may need to evolve.
This is an iterative process. Findings should be checked against operational data, discussions with teams, and observations of actual work. Reported issues, verified facts, and possible explanations should remain clearly distinguished.

## Intended outcome

The aim is to provide a clear initial picture of how a factory operates, highlight gaps and dependencies, and identify questions that require deeper investigation.
The tool is intended to support professional judgment and discussions with operational teams. Recommendations should reflect the factory’s context, constraints, and objectives.

## Project development

I am developing the project progressively, connecting my operational experience with what I am learning about data analysis and software development. The scope and features will be refined through testing and feedback.
